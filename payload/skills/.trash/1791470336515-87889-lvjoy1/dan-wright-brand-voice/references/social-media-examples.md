# Social Media Examples

## Core Principles for Social Posts

**Length**: 1-2 sentences maximum (rarely 3)
**Style**: Short, direct, conversational
**Punctuation**: Never use em dashes. Use commas or periods.
**Exclamation points**: Extremely rare or never
**Format**: Often starts with "From [Event] at @[venue]" or "Here's a photo from..."

## Required Hashtags

ALWAYS include these hashtags:
1. **The venue** (Dan will provide)
2. **The performer** (Dan will provide)
3. **#dwphotony**
4. **Additional relevant hashtags** (2-3 more)

Example hashtag structure:
```
#carnegiehall #performername #dwphotony #classicalmusic #nyc
```

## Instagram/Facebook Posts

### Performance Photo Posts

**Example 1: Simple and Direct**
```
On Wings of Song at @carnegiehall. I love when the wardrobe and the space work together like this.

#carnegiehall #performername #dwphotony #classicalmusic #performancephotography
```

**Why it works:**
- Opens with event and venue
- One observation, simply stated
- 2 sentences total
- Includes all required hashtags

**Example 2: Focus on a Detail**
```
From Mozart's Requiem at Lincoln Center. 60 singers, one incredible night.

#lincolncenter #performername #dwphotony #choralphotography #mozart
```

**Why it works:**
- Specific numbers create interest
- Very brief
- Lets the photo speak
- Required hashtags present

**Example 3: Venue Variety (since Dan shoots Carnegie constantly)**
```
The Smoke Show at Jalopy Theatre in Brooklyn. Sometimes the smaller venues are the most fun to shoot.

#jalopy #performername #dwphotony #brooklynmusic #documentaryphotography
```

**Why it works:**
- Highlights a different venue
- Personal observation without overthinking it
- Still brief

**Example 4: Behind the Scenes**
```
Backstage at The King's Singers rehearsal. This is the part most people don't see.

#carnegiehall #thekingssingers #dwphotony #behindthescenes #choralmusic
```

**Why it works:**
- Different angle from performance shots
- Simple statement
- No need to over-explain

### Minimal Posts (Let the Photo Talk)

**Example 5: Just the Facts**
```
Sunrise Mass at @carnegiehall.

#carnegiehall #performername #dwphotony #choralmusic #nyc
```

**Why it works:**
- Sometimes one line is enough
- The image carries the weight
- Professional and clean

**Example 6: Event + Observation**
```
From Vocal Colors at Lincoln Center. The lighting in this venue never disappoints.

#lincolncenter #performername #dwphotony #performancephotography #nyc
```

**Why it works:**
- Acknowledges the venue without repeating same old Carnegie Hall praise
- Specific about what made it work
- Brief

### Engagement Posts (Use Sparingly)

**Example 7: Genuine Question**
```
Quick question for directors: What matters more in performance photos, technical perfection or authentic emotion?

I've always leaned toward authenticity, but curious what you prioritize.

#performancephotography #theaterlife #dwphotony
```

**Why it works:**
- Real question, not engagement bait
- Still relatively brief (2 sentences)
- Invites actual conversation
- Note: For non-performance posts, still include #dwphotony but venue/performer tags may not apply

## Variety Strategies for Carnegie Hall

Since Dan shoots at Carnegie Hall constantly, here are different angles to avoid repetition:

1. **Focus on the performance/repertoire**: "Mozart's Requiem at Carnegie Hall"
2. **Highlight a detail**: "I love when the wardrobe and the space work together like this"
3. **Behind the scenes**: "Backstage before curtain"
4. **Technical observation**: "The lighting during this piece was perfect"
5. **About the performers**: "60 singers, one incredible night"
6. **Minimal approach**: Just event name + venue tag
7. **Process insight**: "This is the angle I always try to get"
8. **Audience perspective**: "The view from the balcony"

## What NOT to Do

**Too long:**
```
There's something about Weill Recital Hall at Carnegie Hall that never gets old. That chandelier, the acoustics, the way the whole room feels intimate even from the back row. This was from "On Wings of Song" last week, a soprano and pianist collaboration...
```

**Using em dashes:**
```
From Carnegie Hall, one of those venues that, no matter how many times I shoot there, still impresses me.
```

**Too enthusiastic:**
```
AMAZING night at Carnegie Hall!!! The performance was INCREDIBLE and I'm so excited to share these photos with you!!!
```

**Too corporate:**
```
Dan Wright Photography is pleased to present images from a recent engagement at Carnegie Hall featuring exceptional performances by talented musicians.
```

**Missing required hashtags:**
```
Beautiful performance tonight.

#music #photography
```
(Missing: venue, performer, #dwphotony)

## Quick Checklist for Social Posts

- [ ] Is it 1-2 sentences (max 3)?
- [ ] Did I avoid em dashes?
- [ ] Is it conversational but not overly casual?
- [ ] Would I actually say this out loud?
- [ ] Does it let the photo do most of the talking?
- [ ] Am I varying my approach if it's another Carnegie Hall post?
- [ ] Do I have ALL required hashtags: venue, performer, #dwphotony?
- [ ] Do I have 2-3 additional relevant hashtags?
