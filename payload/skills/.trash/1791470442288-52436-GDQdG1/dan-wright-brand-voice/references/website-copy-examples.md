# Website Copy Examples

## Homepage / About Section

### Example 1: Hero Section

```
**Headline:**
Documentary-Style Photography for Performing Arts

**Subheadline:**
I capture the authentic moments that make your performances unforgettable without getting in the way.

**Body:**
Whether you're performing at Carnegie Hall or a neighborhood theater, your show deserves photography that honors the artistry on stage. I specialize in documentary-style coverage that captures real emotion, not posed moments. My approach is simple: stay unobtrusive, anticipate the key moments, and deliver images that feel as alive as the performance itself.
```

**Why it works:**
- Headline is clear and benefit-focused
- Subheadline addresses the main differentiator
- Body paragraph explains the how and why
- Speaks directly to the client ("your show", "your performances")
- Avoids jargon and pretension

### Example 2: About Section

```
**About Dan**

I'm a photographer based in NYC, specializing in documentary-style coverage of performing arts. Over the past [X] years, I've photographed hundreds of performances at venues like Carnegie Hall, Lincoln Center, and intimate theaters across the city.

My approach is straightforward: I stay out of the way. No flash during performances, no interrupting for posed shots, no distracting your performers or your audience. I capture what actually happens the authentic moments, the real emotion, the performance as it unfolds.

This documentary style means you get images that truly represent your work. Not what we staged for the camera, but what your audience experienced.

When you're ready to document your next performance, let's talk.
```

**Why it works:**
- First person throughout
- Specific credentials without bragging
- Explains the philosophy clearly
- Three punchy sentences in paragraph 2 create emphasis
- Em dash used for elaboration
- Soft CTA at the end

## Services Page

### Example 3: Service Description

```
**Performance Photography**

I document your live performances from rehearsal to final bow. This includes:
- Full coverage of dress rehearsals and performances
- Backstage and candid moments
- Wide shots establishing the scene and intimate close-ups
- Professional editing and color correction
- High-resolution digital delivery

**What makes my approach different:** I work documentary-style, which means I'm capturing real moments as they happen. No staged shots during the performance, no interrupting the flow. Your performers stay in the moment, and you get images that authentically represent what your audience experienced.

**Best for:** Theater companies, dance ensembles, choirs, orchestras, and performing arts organizations that want professional documentation without disrupting the performance.
```

**Why it works:**
- Clear structure (what's included, what's different, who it's for)
- Bullet points used appropriately for scanning
- "What makes my approach different" directly addresses the question
- "Best for" helps clients self-select
- Avoids overselling or hype

## Voice Notes for Website Copy

- **Clarity over cleverness**: Say what you mean directly
- **Benefit-focused**: Lead with what the client gets, not what you do
- **Hierarchy**: Use headlines, subheads, and bullets to aid scanning
- **Action-oriented**: Tell people what to do next (but gently)
- **Specific**: Name venues, types of performances, actual processes
- **Conversational**: Write like you'd explain this to someone at a coffee shop
- **Trust-building**: Show you understand their needs and challenges
- **No hype**: Avoid superlatives and exaggeration
- **White space**: Break up text, make it scannable
- **Empathy**: Show you understand what matters to performing arts directors
