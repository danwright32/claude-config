## Guidelines for Project Brief Creation

- **Preserve the user's intent** - don't add objectives they didn't mention
- **Capture everything** - even vague signals belong in the brief
- **Flag ambiguity** - put unclear items in Open Questions
- **Stay under 500 lines** - this is a synthesis, not a spec
- **Use the user's language** - don't over-formalize their ideas
- **Don't make execution decisions** - that happens in Discovery
- **Include surface signals** - note which execution surfaces seem relevant even if uncertain
