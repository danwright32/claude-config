Disk clean, the review page

The page /disk-clean opens in Chrome so you can tick what it removes (#1807). Settled over five rounds, approved by Dan on 2026-10-28.

The settled design. A new product, whose page does not exist yet. Every frame is drawn at its own width, from the committed fixture the built page renders too. The build is done when it scores 100% against this file on both halves.

Requirements

  • 1 Every scan item is listed by its kind, each with a tick box, and none is ticked when the page opens
  • 2 Items it will not remove are greyed, with their reason
  • 3 An item holding a credential, or kept in iCloud Drive, is marked
  • 4 The page reads in light and in dark
  • 5 Copy selection, pinned to the foot, copies what is ticked back to the session

Decisions

  1. D1 One list: each kind is a heading row with its total on the right, its items beneath, nothing boxed. Round 1, chosen over a bordered block per kind and a flat table. Requirement 1. Checked on f1, f2, f3: Each kind is an h2 heading row followed by its item rows, with that kind's total in the same row, and no kind is drawn inside a bordered box.
  2. D2 Within a kind, the items it will not remove come after every item you can tick. Round 2, chosen over mixing them in and over one Kept back section at the foot. Requirement 2. Checked on f1: Within every kind, no row whose tick box is enabled comes after a row whose tick box is disabled.
  3. D3 Kinds come largest first by what can be freed, with Tools not used in 180 days and Large personal files always last. Round 3: the biggest wins first, but your own files never lead, since they cannot come back. Requirement 1. Checked on f1: The kinds other than Tools not used in 180 days and Large personal files appear in falling order of their totals, and those two are the last two kinds, in that order.
  4. D4 Within a kind, the items you can tick are largest first, and a size the scan could not measure comes last. Follows round 3's largest first down to the rows. Requirement 1. Checked on f1: Within every kind, the enabled rows' sizes do not rise from one row to the next, and a row reading Size unknown follows every measured one.
  5. D5 A row is one line: path, then its reason or detail, then its marks, wrapping only when too long. Round 4, chosen over stacking each on its own line and over two lines. Requirement 2. Checked on f1: In a row with a note and no marks, the path and the note sit on one line at 1100 wide.
  6. D6 Blue is used only on ticked boxes and the Copy selection button. Round 5: colour only on what you do. Requirement 5. Checked on f2, f3: The Copy selection button's background and the ticked boxes' accent are blue, and no other element is blue.
  7. D7 The two marks, Holds a credential and Removes it from iCloud on every device, are amber, and red is not used anywhere. Round 5: a risky row is seen before it is ticked; red stays for something actually wrong. Requirement 3. Checked on f1, f3: Every mark has an amber background and border, and no element on the page is red.
  8. D8 A mark is said once: an item's note never repeats what its mark says. Every fact once per screen (L605); the iCloud note repeated its mark in the rounds. Requirement 3. Checked on f1: The iCloud item's note reads in iCloud Drive and does not say every device.
  9. D9 A bar fixed to the foot holds the count and size of what is ticked, or Nothing selected, and Copy selection. Held the same through every round. Requirement 5. Checked on f1, f2: The foot bar reads Nothing selected in f1 and 4 selected, 35.3 GB in f2, beside a Copy selection button.
  10. D10 Dark keeps every role: a dark surface, light text, the same blue action and amber marks, tuned for the dark. The issue asks for light and dark; the colours carry the roles round 5 chose. Requirement 4. Checked on f3: In f3 the page background is dark, the text is light, Copy selection is blue and the marks are amber.

Exceptions Dan approved

None. A deliberate difference from this design is a new design round, or an exception Dan approves in his own words, recorded here.
